## About the Event

Engineering Her Way, an online seminar hosted by the IEEE MIST Student Branch and the IEEE MIST WIE SB Affinity Group,
underscored that behind every achievement is a story of late nights, self-doubt, sacrifices and challenges that often
go unseen. The women we celebrate did not reach their positions through easy paths — they faced obstacles, questioned
themselves, worked harder and continued moving forward.

This seminar is dedicated to the women in engineering who are still learning, still struggling and still shining.

The lessons and experiences presented serve as an inspiration to all, transcending gender boundaries.
